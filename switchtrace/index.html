Outlet notes.
On your wrist.

A small directory for labels you want to remember. Name an outlet on iPhone, then save its label and a breaker number on your Watch after a charging observation.

SwitchTrace 0.1.0 is available free on the App Store. Requires a compatible paired iPhone and Apple Watch. Get SwitchTrace on the App Store.

1

Open both apps

Connect the iPhone charger, name the outlet and start a trace. Keep both apps active and reachable for a current observation.

2

Save a note

After a fresh charging-loss observation, choose a number from 1 to 200 with the Digital Crown and save the label.

3

Find it later

Saved notes stay in the Watch directory. Removing one asks you to confirm the selected entry.

An observation, with clear limits.

A phone's charging state does not verify a breaker mapping or prove a circuit is off. Charger removal, battery charging management and delayed updates can affect the report. SwitchTrace is not electrical test equipment.

Keep both apps active and reachable for a current observation. Reopen both apps when the reading is stale or unavailable. Never use SwitchTrace to decide whether wiring is safe to touch.

Small by design

No app account, advertising, developer telemetry service or cloud note sync. Notes are stored locally on your Watch. The phone sends its charging observation to the paired Watch through Apple's framework. Apple-managed device backups may include your notes.

Designed for iPhone with iOS 17 or later and a compatible paired Apple Watch with watchOS 10 or later.
