What Still does today

Still reviews metadata for files and folders you explicitly select through Apple’s document picker. References and review progress are remembered between launches in private app storage on your iPhone. If a provider withdraws access, Still asks you to select the item again.

Still does not delete or move files held by iCloud Drive or other document providers. “Set Aside” creates a review decision; complete any change later in Apple Files or the provider’s app.

Private by design

Your files are not our business.

Still has no account, ads, or analytics. Selected file details are processed on your iPhone and are not uploaded.
