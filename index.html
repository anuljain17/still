Permission before indexing

You choose what Foldrobe can see.

iOS does not allow a third-party app to scan the entire phone. You grant access to folders through Apple’s secure system picker.

✓

What Foldrobe does today

Foldrobe recursively indexes accessible files in each folder you allow. It watches for changes while active and securely remembers each folder in private app storage so it can catch up after a relaunch.

Search & Manage can copy or move a selection, keep cut files ready for Paste, rename one file, or delete selected files and filename matches. Existing destination files are never overwritten.

Private by design

Your files are not our business.

Foldrobe has no account, ads, or behavioral tracking. File metadata and image previews are processed on your iPhone and are not uploaded.
